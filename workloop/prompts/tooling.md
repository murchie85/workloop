STAGE: TOOLING ACQUISITION

Just-in-time, ~10 minutes. Read formalisation.md. For each increment or
problem where a tool could remove a step, search for candidate tools
(web search is allowed), evaluate in parallel with subagents where useful,
and decide. Tools are cattle not pets: anything adopted must sit behind a
thin interface {NAME} owns, named in one place.

Stop condition is a decision, not a reading list.

Write tooling.md:

---
adopted: [names]
parked: [names]
build: [what we build ourselves]
---
## Runs
| problem | candidates | decision | why (one line) |
## Interfaces
(for each adopted tool: the thin wrapper name and where it lives)
## Misses
(what was searched and found nothing useful; keep it, it is the tool map)

Then give {NAME} the adopted/parked/build lists only.
